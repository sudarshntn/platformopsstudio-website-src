This page describes, in plain terms, how Platform Ops Studio ("we", "I") handles information collected through platformopsstudio.com.

## Information we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, phone number, and message you provide, solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Server logs kept by the hosting provider record standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested URL) for the purpose of operating the site and diagnosing issues. Those logs are retained per the hosting provider's default retention policy.

## Cookies and storage

This site uses a single first-party entry in the browser's `localStorage` to remember your response to the cookie notice — either that you accepted or declined analytics.

No analytics scripts and no advertising or third-party tracking cookies are loaded by this site. If that changes, this policy will be updated first and the cookie banner will surface the change before anything new is loaded.

## Your choices

- **Withdraw analytics consent.** Click _Cookie preferences_ in the footer to open the banner again and change your choice.
- **Delete stored data.** Clearing site data in your browser removes the consent preference entry.
- **Contact form data.** Email the address below to request deletion of any message you sent through the contact form.
